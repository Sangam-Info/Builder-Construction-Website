01 / THE PROJECT

An address built
for business.

Alpha One Business Centre is a landmark commercial development conceived for Junagadh's growing retail and corporate scene. Its contemporary façade, generous glazing and landscaped terraces give every unit a confident street presence.

Positioned at a busy junction with connections toward Rajkot, Zanzarda and Junagadh City, it offers high footfall, easy access and well-planned front and underground parking — an ideal home for showrooms, service brands and offices alike.
